Privacy at Spiralweb Papers

A short note on visiting this publication site and getting in touch.

Updated 27 September 2026

← Spiralweb Papers · Library

Who is responsible?

Spiralweb Papers is a separately held publication site stewarded by Lars A. Engberg. For questions about personal information connected with Papers, write to contact@spiralweb.earth and mark the message “Papers privacy”. The address uses the shared Spiralweb domain; please do not send sensitive personal or field material by ordinary email.

This notice concerns this publication site. The Association’s membership, collections and other activities have a separate privacy notice.

When you visit

You can read Papers without an account. Cloudflare delivers the pages and may process technical request data, including an IP address, to deliver and protect the site. Papers does not use advertising trackers or ask you to accept optional tracking cookies. Cloudflare may use security measures where needed.

The limited processing described here serves the legitimate interests of keeping a public library available and secure, understanding demand for its PDFs, and answering readers and corrections. Cloudflare and the service that delivers our email process information needed for those services. Their technical records are subject to their own retention settings; Papers does not use those records to build visitor profiles.

Some older publications load fonts from Google. Opening one of those pages may cause your browser to contact Google directly. We are reviewing those pages.

PDF downloads

A small server-side counter may record a PDF’s path and whether a visitor came from another page on this site, from an external site or without a usable referrer. It counts requests to understand which publications are used; it does not deliberately store names, IP addresses, full external referrer URLs or user-agent strings in that counter. Cloudflare’s ordinary delivery and security processing is separate from this count.

If you write to us

An email provides your address and whatever information you choose to share. It is used to answer your question or handle a correction, and kept only while needed for that exchange or a justified record of the correction. The site’s Library search runs in your browser; it does not submit your search terms to a Papers search service.

Your choices

For access to, correction of or deletion of personal information connected with Papers, or to object to its use, contact contact@spiralweb.earth and mark the message “Papers privacy”. You can also complain to Datatilsynet. Published material about people or places is handled through its own consent and correction process; a request will be considered in its specific context.
