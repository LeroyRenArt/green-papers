From reading to practice

Practice & tools

A ditch dries out earlier than it did last year. A grower, a neighbour and a biologist may have different explanations. Before choosing what to do, they can bring their observations together, agree on a question and decide when to return.

From first observation to return

  1. Write down what you noticed. Record where and when, and keep what you saw separate from what you think might explain it. Begin with the Field Diary.
  2. Choose a question together. What do you want to find out, and what might change your minds? Agree what to try or follow, who will do what, and when you will meet again. Choose a format if you need a record of an agreement or decision.
  3. Return and see what happened. What changed for the place and for the people doing the work? What remains unclear? Agree what to use locally and what participants may wish to share. Read about place-based learning spirals.
