Living function

Capacity remains in contact with consequence.

Spiralweb carries several kinds of movement at once. Money may support a steward or a place. An observation may become a decision. A relationship may open an agreement. Field experience may correct a public protocol. Each movement has its own conditions, authority, memory and return path.

This protocol offers a shared way to make those passages understandable. It begins with the relationship that exists, locates who may decide, keeps different flows separately readable, and connects action with the consequences that follow.

A flow becomes part of a living architecture when the people and places carrying its consequences can shape its passage, read its effects, receive a useful return and correct what happens next.

What moves

Different flows retain their different meanings.

Flow names movement across a relationship. Money, knowledge, attention and responsibility retain distinct meanings, rights, burdens and possibilities.

Resources

Money, materials, time and practical capacity

A resource flow carries a stated purpose, lawful route, decision, period and accounting trace.

Knowledge and evidence

Observation, interpretation, hypothesis and learning

A knowledge flow carries provenance, uncertainty, consent, claim boundaries and a route for correction.

Attention and relationship

Contact, visibility, care and continuity

An attention flow carries tempo, hosting work, privacy, language and the capacity to receive further contact.

Responsibility and decision

Mandate, commitment, release and answerability

A responsibility flow names who holds the next decision, who carries the consequence and when a Return is due.

The recurring movement

Seven movements connect input with Return.

The sequence is a public orientation. A real process may pause, revisit an earlier movement or discover that the original question has changed.

01

Receive

Name what has arrived and acknowledge who now holds it.

02

Locate

Place it in the actual relationship, context and standing.

03

Qualify

Clarify purpose, authority, capacity, evidence and visibility.

04

Decide

A named human or legitimate body makes the bounded judgement.

05

Move

Act, release, support, wait or pause through the agreed route.

06

Remember

Keep the proportionate financial, relational and situated trace.

07

Return

Bring consequence back and correct what comes next.

The membrane

Passage becomes legitimate through relationship and boundary.

Spiralweb uses the membrane as a practical grammar for passage. A membrane allows movement where the relationship can carry it and preserves the distinctions that keep authority, privacy, knowledge and resources properly placed.

  1. RelationshipWho is actually in relationship with whom?
  2. Purpose and standingWhy is this moving, and who may observe, propose, decide, receive, share or refuse?
  3. Capacity and burdenCan the field, steward and institutional holder receive the movement within their actual carrying capacity?
  4. Evidence and uncertaintyWhat was observed, interpreted, inferred, decided and later experienced? Evidence standing follows the Grounding Protocol, keeping observation, inference, claim, proposal and uncertainty distinguishable and open to correction.
  5. Custody and visibilityWhere does memory belong, and what may travel into another access layer?
  6. Return, pause and exitWhat comes back, and how can the movement slow, change, close or be corrected?

The protocol uses three related grammars to hold different dimensions of the same passage.

Resource flow

Three streams remain separately readable.

Resource flows communicate and affect one another. Each protects a different condition of the shared work, and movement between them becomes visible through explicit human governance.

Stream A

Land and Ecology

Soil, water, plants, habitat, food, tools and locally relevant ecological work.

Stream B

Steward Viability

Livelihood, time, rest, travel, learning and the capacity of people carrying continuity.

Stream C

Coordination and Governance

Agreements, finance, translation, documentation, governance, learning and responsible institutional holding.

Here, Land and Ecology, Steward Viability and Coordination and Governance name resource destinations. This vocabulary follows Capacity, Contact, and the Emerging Stewardship Economy. The Penguin Dashboard separately reads the related conditions of Land / Life, Steward Viability and Governance / Commons.

Association application · internal working practice

Flow Desk: One Card · One Envelope · One Return

This public protocol makes the Association’s governing movement inspectable while Flow Desk remains its internal working architecture. Spiralweb Stewardship Association applies the protocol through a light relational practice that gives every active responsibility a current position, a legitimate memory location and a named Return.

Each active responsibility names a primary human holder, a backup and an honest response horizon.

Learning Return

The public shelf fills when practice has something ready to carry.

A Field Return is bounded learning that has come back from a real place, relationship or institutional practice. It may preserve an observation, uncertainty, disagreement, changed decision, pause, unintended consequence or correction to the public architecture.

Before learning travels, the responsible people ask:

  • What can travel with consent and useful context?
  • What remains locally held or protected?
  • What is an observation here and a hypothesis elsewhere?
  • Which public paper, protocol or tool may need correction?

The Field Returns shelf on Spiralweb Papers is presently open and waiting. Its emptiness is a truthful stage of the work.

Human judgement and AI

Assistance can expand while responsibility remains situated.

AI may help organise, compare, retrieve, translate, transcribe and draft. Consequential movement returns to a named human or legitimate body with standing to judge, decide, release, pause and repair.

The Sophia Lumen Protocol holds the co-inquiry practice. The Correction Loop holds accountability where AI-assisted work becomes consequential. The Last Impulse remains human.

Minimum public grammar

Enough memory for responsibility and correction.

A proportionate record keeps nine things separately readable. The form, language and rhythm can change with context.

OriginWhere and from whom did the movement arise?
PurposeWhat living function or agreed question does it serve?
StandingWho may know, propose, decide, receive or share?
DecisionWho made the bounded judgement, and when?
MovementWhat actually moved, through which route?
MemoryWhere does the financial, relational or situated trace belong?
ConsequenceWhat became visible after the movement?
ReturnWhat came back to the relationship?
Next decisionWhat continues, changes, waits, pauses or closes?

Candidate tests

The first cycles will correct the protocol.

The protocol will be read through practical questions:

  • Can each active responsibility reach a named human?
  • Can the people concerned see and correct what is being held?
  • Can the three resource streams remain visible alongside formal accounts?
  • Can field memory support learning without creating a second reporting burden?
  • Can quiet, uncertainty, pause and waiting retain their distinct meanings?
  • Can consequence change a release, agreement, public statement, tool or protocol?
  • Does the Return deepen contact and make the next movement more truthful?

Field experience may produce a revised candidate. Repeated situated use may later establish validation for a defined purpose and context.

Closing proposition

Flow is held through relationship. Movement becomes legible through responsibility. Memory preserves consequence. Return keeps the architecture capable of learning.
