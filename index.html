Who does the work when the grant ends?

A council can close the books on a project while the soil still needs tending and the water follows its own seasons. At holde jorden levende follows the work that begins after a change in land use. Municipal Work as Nature follows the work that slips out of the accounts and falls to staff. Read them together and ask who has the time, the means and the authority to keep going.

Land, water and habitats

What needs room at the edge of a field?

A field margin is small enough to walk along and large enough to raise questions about the whole catchment. Living Landscapes draws attention to species and habitats. The Field Diary gives you a way to follow what changes when you return.

Water, drought and catchments

Care, work and economy

What leaves a place as value, and what stays behind as a cost?

Food and income can travel far from a farm. The work of tending the soil and the consequences of water scarcity remain there. The stewardship economy inquiry follows where those costs land and asks how the work might become a livelihood.

Stewardship economy

Knowledge, judgement and AI

What does someone who has been there a long time know?

An observation from a place can unsettle a professional explanation. Knowing From the Ground makes room for that experience. A learning spiral gives people a reason to return and see whether the explanation still holds.

Knowledge from place

Communities and institutions

What can a place make an institution reconsider?

A funder can give people time to do the work. A public authority may have the power to change an agreement. How can both take what people learn at the place seriously and make room for those who live with the consequences?
