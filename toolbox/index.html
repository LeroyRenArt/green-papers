A shared toolbox, a workbench of your own

The toolbox holds reusable material. Your workbench is the selection of questions, observations, sources and corrections you keep for your own inquiry. A group can build a workbench together under its own agreements.

The tools have a regenerative orientation: care for living conditions, the people carrying that care, and workable shared responsibilities. What that means becomes clearer through use, evidence and correction.

Connected to the wider habitat

The Protocol Habitat holds the foundations, methods and protocols behind these tools. Flow Desk holds the Association’s coordination of agreed work. The five PG Ledger formats help record observations, actions, decisions and resource flows when useful.

This collection begins small. Further tools and selected learning from use can be added as the work develops. Shared accounts carry their context, contributors’ permissions, uncertainty and corrections.
