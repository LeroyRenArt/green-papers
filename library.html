      A note on formation and stewardship

      The First Spiral grew out of an extended artistic inquiry in 2025, gathered through Gaia Bloom and its thirty-nine scrolls. Its themes emerged through poetry, embodied experience, and dialogue with AI language systems. The first twenty papers were later revisited and substantially reworked, retaining much of their original substance while developing their connections with the emerging Spiralweb architecture. Moral Biology names one line of inquiry that emerged in that process; it is not presented as an established biological science. Public claims remain open to research, comparison, and correction.

      The resulting texts are relationally formed. They are neither presented as the work of an isolated sovereign author nor as autonomous AI authorship. Each public version is selected, corrected, released, and held under named human stewardship and responsibility.

      The papers are offered to be read on whether they hold — and to remain open to correction where they do not.
