Important rule

When this three-stream frame is used, the streams are non-compensatory. A strong reading in one stream does not justify weakness in another.

Stream A records Land and Ecology flows.
Stream B records Steward Viability flows.
Stream C records Governance and Collaboration flows.

Consent and care

Reference the receipt or evidence where available. Where photographs, names, schools, or minors are involved, note privacy or consent. Consent is ongoing and revisable.

If a transfer crosses an internal threshold, record the reviewed-by identifier and ensure dual review. If anything is unclear, log it as a question rather than as a fact.

Remember: Connect source to recipient, purpose, evidence, and lived effect. One line per transaction. This sheet does not itself activate a PG Ledger or replace formal accounting.
