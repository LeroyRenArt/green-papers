Important rule

Do not force green. Unknown, yellow, or red is useful honesty.

Green may justify a small next step.
Yellow usually calls for adjustment.
Red calls for protection, pause, or repair.
Unknown means there is not enough evidence to make the reading; it is distinct from Yellow.
